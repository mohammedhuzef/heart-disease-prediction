UNDERSTANDING ERROR TYPES IN CLINICAL PRACTICE

FALSE POSITIVES (12 CASES)

What it means: The algorithm flags high risk, but formal diagnostic tests find healthy cardiac vessels.
Clinical impact: Low harm. The patient undergoes non-invasive follow-up (stress echocardiogram or lifestyle counseling). Inconvenient, but medically safe.

FALSE NEGATIVES (7 CASES)

What it means: The algorithm estimates low risk, but the patient actually has underlying vascular narrowing.
Clinical impact: High harm. Might provide false reassurance and delay angiographic evaluation. That is why CardioCheck insists: Never rely on model output over clinical symptoms!

UNDERSTANDING KEY CLINICAL INDICATORS

PLAIN NON-MEDICAL LANGUAGE
CARDIAC RHYTHM 32.9% INFLUENCE

ST Segment Slope

During an exercise stress test, your ECG's ST wave can slope upward, stay flat, or slope downward. A flat or downsloping ST wave indicates the heart muscle may not be receiving sufficient oxygen during exertion.

SYMPTOM PRESENTATION 15.3% INFLUENCE

Chest Pain Type

Pain isn't always typical crushing pressure. Surprisingly, in cardiac datasets, “asymptomatic” ischemia (ASY) frequently corresponds to advanced disease because silent blockage progresses without early warning.

PHYSICAL EXERTION 13.5% INFLUENCE

Exercise-Induced Angina

Experiencing chest tightness or breathlessness specifically during exercise suggests that when the heart works harder, narrowed coronary arteries cannot deliver enough blood flow.

ELECTRICAL DEFICIT 8.7% INFLUENCE

Oldpeak (ST Depression)

Measures how far the ST segment dips below the normal resting baseline. Higher depression values (e.g. > 1.5 mm) strongly correlate with temporary lack of blood flow in subendocardial tissue.

AEROBIC CAPACITY 7.4% INFLUENCE

Maximum Heart Rate (MaxHR)

The highest pulse rate achieved during peak exercise. An inability to raise the heart rate to age-predicted norms (chronotropic incompetence) often flags underlying cardiovascular strain.

VASCULAR HEALTH 4.5% INFLUENCE

Age & Cholesterol

Arterial stiffness increases naturally with age, while elevated circulating cholesterol particles contribute to plaque buildup (atherosclerosis) inside coronary vessels over years.

PREVENTATIVE HEALTH AWARENESS

EDUCATIONAL ONLY
🩺

Blood Pressure Matters

High blood pressure is often nicknamed the “silent killer” because it damages arterial linings without noticeable pain. Regular monitoring helps protect vessel elasticity and heart function.

📅

Regular Follow-ups Matter

Routine checkups with a healthcare provider let you track trends over time. Early lifestyle modifications or treatments are far more effective than reacting after an acute cardiac event.

🚨

Know Your Symptoms

Watch for sudden shortness of breath, unexplained cold sweats, radiation of discomfort to the jaw, neck, or back, and extreme fatigue during simple physical activities.

WARNING 01 🚫

Not a Diagnosis Tool

CardioCheck is strictly an academic decision-support demonstration. It computes risk approximations from statistical patterns and cannot diagnose coronary artery disease, ischemia, or structural heart defects.

WARNING 02 📊

Dataset Limitations

The model is trained on curated archival samples (UCI Heart Disease repository). The dataset size (approx. 918 instances across Cleveland, Hungarian, and Swiss cohorts) may not capture rare pathologies or modern clinical variations.

WARNING 03 ⚖️

Possible Historical Bias

Historical clinical datasets frequently underrepresent specific demographic groups, young populations, and atypical presentations (such as women presenting with microvascular coronary disease without classic angina).

WARNING 04 👨‍⚕️

Cannot Replace a Doctor

No machine learning model can substitute for physical auscultation, clinical history, coronary angiography, echocardiography, or the nuanced clinical judgment of certified healthcare practitioners.

WARNING 05 👶

Not for Children or Pregnancy

Model parameters are calibrated strictly for adult populations under non-emergent screening conditions. It is not designed or validated for pediatric patients, pregnancy-related cases, or acute trauma.

PURPOSE 06 🎯

Intended Educational Use

Educational demonstration and preliminary risk-awareness support only. Developed as a student portfolio project to showcase how human-centered design and explainable AI can make clinical algorithms transparent.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Pipeline architecture, dependencies, and reproducibility
SCIKIT-LEARN v1.6.1

Model Architecture

  • Estimator: Random Forest Classifier (200 estimators, max_depth=10, min_samples_split=10)
  • Preprocessor: Scikit-learn Pipeline with ColumnTransformer
  • Imputation: Median imputation for numerical features
  • Scaling: StandardScaler normalization
  • Encoding: OneHotEncoder (handle_unknown='ignore') for categorical values

Design & Accessibility

  • Style: Bold Neo-Brutalism with 3px solid black borders and 6px offset shadows
  • Color System: Cream #F7F3E8 background, near-black #111111 borders, high contrast AAA
  • Accessibility: 44px+ tap targets, color + icon redundant signaling, keyboard accessible
  • Deployment: Standalone web application with Python API server integration
